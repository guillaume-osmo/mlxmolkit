Extraction: tools/import_pm7_params.py; 40-element subset matching the PM6 registry.
H,Li,Be,B,C,N,O,F,Na,Mg,Al,Si,P,S,Cl,K,Ca,Sc,Ti,V,Cr,Mn,Fe,Co,Ni,Cu,
Zn,Ga,Ge,As,Se,Br,Rb,Sr,Cd,In,Sn,Sb,Te,I.
Includes PM7 one-center parameters, orbital and tail exponents, Gaussian
core terms, and PM7 diatomic alpha/chi parameters. Zero-valued fields are
explicit zeros, never fallback PM6 values. Physical constants use the
current CODATA fpcref(1,:) in src/conref_C.F90.
Integral feathering and core-core source: src/integrals/mndod.F90 and ccrep.F90.
Correction sources: src/corrections/H_bond_correction_PM6_DH_Dispersion.F90,
Hydrogen_bond_corrections.F90, H_bond_correction_EH_plus.F90,
H_bond_correction_bits.F90, set_up_dentate.F90, and src/compfg.F90.
Transition-metal initialization: src/integrals/sp_two_electron.F90,
src/models/calpar.F90 and src/integrals/mndod.F90 (inighd, eiscor, ddpo, rotatd).
Valence populations: src/models/parameters_C.F90 (ios + iop + iod).
